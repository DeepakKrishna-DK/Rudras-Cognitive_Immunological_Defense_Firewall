# RUDRAS — DEFENSIVE USE LICENSE ADDENDUM

## Permitted Use

This software is licensed **for DEFENSIVE USE ONLY** on networks and systems
for which you have explicit written authorisation from the network or system owner.

Permitted uses include:
- Monitoring and protecting your own organisation's infrastructure
- Academic research in controlled, isolated lab environments with approval
- Authorised penetration testing under a signed Rules of Engagement document
- Government / law-enforcement operations with lawful authority

## Prohibited Use

You **MUST NOT** deploy or operate Rudras in any of the following circumstances:

1. On networks, hosts, or systems you do not own or have written authorisation to monitor
2. As part of any offensive, intrusive, or denial-of-service operation
3. To capture, store, or analyse traffic of individuals without their knowledge or legal
   authority (wire-tap laws, GDPR Art. 5, ECPA, etc.)
4. To facilitate espionage, intellectual-property theft, or cyber-crime

## Data Retention

Captured evidence and forensic artefacts collected by Rudras are subject to data-minimisation
obligations. The default retention period is **90 days**. Administrators are responsible for
ensuring retention periods comply with applicable law and organisational policy.
